The market doesn't beat you. Your impatience does.

Consider TCS trading at Rs 2290.0. It is down 31.64% from its peak, making it a prime candidate for retail traders looking for a massive recovery. But look closer at the quantitative data: the Average Directional Index (ADX) is sitting at a sluggish 17.4, and the RSI is at a neutral 46.8.

What does this mean? There is no trend. Yet, retail traders frequently fall into 'Bull Traps' here.

Why? Because of FOMO. Traders see a minor green candle and rush in, projecting their hopes onto a flat chart. They place their stop-losses in tight clusters just below the recent breakout levels. When the price reverses, the psychological cycle begins:
1. Shock: 'But the breakout looked so clean!'
2. Denial: 'I will hold, it is just a healthy retest.'
3. Panic: The price slips, hits the stop-loss clusters, and cascades lower.
