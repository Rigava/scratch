## 💼 Draft 2: LinkedIn Post
The hardest part of trading isn't the math. It's managing your own psychology during a quiet market.

Take TCS, currently trading at Rs 2290. On paper, it looks stable. But look closer at the quantitative indicators: the RSI is at 46.8 (neutral) and the ADX is a sleepy 17.4. This low ADX tells us one crucial thing: there is no strong trend.

Yet, this is exactly where the 'Bull Trap' is set.

Impatient retail traders, desperate for action, mistake noise for a breakout. They enter long positions out of FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out), placing their stop-losses in dense clusters just below the resistance line. 

When the lack of real volume causes the price to reverse, the psychological cycle begins:
1. Shock: 'Wait, it was breaking out!'
2. Denial: 'It's just testing support, it will bounce.'
3. Panic: The price slips, triggers the stop-loss cluster, and cascades downward.

As quant traders, our edge lies in discipline. We use indicators like ADX to filter out low-probability environments, protecting our capital from emotional traps.
