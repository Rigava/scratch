This can force them to cover, pushing the price even higher – a classic #TrappedTrader scenario. Bulls, while in a favorable trend, face the psychological trap of complacency or chasing without proper risk management. A disciplined trader would focus on their entry criteria, predefined exit stop losses, and position sizing, irrespective of the noise.
