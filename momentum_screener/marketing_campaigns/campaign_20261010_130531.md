Loss aversion is arguably the most lethal cognitive bias in financial markets. Kahneman and Tversky proved that the pain of losing is psychologically twice as powerful as the pleasure of gaining. In trading, this manifests as an insidious habit: refusing to take a small loss, holding onto hope, and praying the market returns to breakeven. Consider BHEL trading at Rs 430.85. The technical profile is structured and dominant: pushing 52-week highs, maintaining a shallow drawdown of just 3.51%, carrying an RSI of 59.6, and confirming a fresh bullish MACD crossover.
