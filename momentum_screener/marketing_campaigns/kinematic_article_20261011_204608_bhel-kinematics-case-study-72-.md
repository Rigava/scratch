### 💡 The Mental Model: Understanding Market Kinematics

Think of a ball thrown upwards. At its peak, its *position* is the highest. But its *velocity* (speed) has slowed to zero, and *acceleration* (the force pulling it down) is at its strongest. The ball is about to fall, even though it's still at its highest point!

This is Market Kinematics. It's not just about **where price is** (the ball's position), but **how price is moving** (velocity), and **how the *rate of change* is evolving** (acceleration/deceleration).

We analyze the "derivatives of change" over T-2, T-1, and T (today) to see:
*   **Velocity:** Is momentum building or fading?
*   **Acceleration:** Is that momentum picking up speed or slowing down?
*   **Inertia:** Are buyers/sellers trapped?
*   **Gravity/Lift:** Are institutional forces pulling price up or down?

This multi-session view reveals the hidden forces that single-day charts completely miss.

### 📉 Case Study A: The Bull Trap – BHEL's "Peak Velocity" Exhaustion

Let's look at **BHEL**. Just a few sessions ago, it hit a 52-week high, alluring many with a perceived "breakout."
*   **What happened:** Price surged towards Rs 455.95, tempting breakout buyers. But then, a sharp rejection. BHEL closed at Rs 429.60, down **-4.21%** on heavy volume.
*   **The Kinematic Insight:** This wasn't just a red day. It was an **"Expansion Failure"** and a **"Bull Trap"** confirmed by multi-session kinematics. Price hit a high, but its *upward velocity* decelerated sharply, met by *heavy selling pressure* (the equivalent of hitting the brakes hard). The large selling wick indicated institutional distribution.
*   **Who got trapped?** Retail traders chasing the "breakout" at the absolute peak, only to see price reverse and fall back *below* the breakout level. They bought at peak momentum, just as smart money was exiting.
*   **Why it's dangerous:** These trapped buyers now represent overhead supply, making it harder for the stock to recover.
