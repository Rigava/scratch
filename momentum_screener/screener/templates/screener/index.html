Behavioral Analyst Perspective: Buying/Selling Capitulations What this means: Captures extreme emotional extremes where panic selling meets capitulation bottoms, or speculative FOMO meets blow-off tops. These events represent a major transfer of liquidity from retail to institutions.
Deterministic Logic: Scans for days with extreme volume expansion ($Z_{Volume} > 1.2$) and range expansion ($Z_{Spread} > 0.8$).
  • Bearish Capitulation: Stock is below EMA10, but closes near the high of its range ($CLV > 0.2$), showing aggressive buying absorbed the panic dump.

How to interpret Score: Higher score signifies an intense volume climax on a wide day. Look for Bearish Capitulations as explosive bottom reversals, and Bullish Capitulations as exhaustion tops.

Volatility Contraction Pattern (VCP)

Evaluating...
Hedge Fund PM Perspective: Volatility & Volume Contraction What this means: Before a major breakout occurs, supply must be fully digested. This is seen when the price range gets extremely narrow and trading volume drops to a trickle, showing that there are no sellers left in the market.
Deterministic Logic: Triggers when the stock is trading within 5% of its recent 20-day high. It measures Volatility Compression (whether 5-day return standard deviation is smaller than 20-day standard deviation) and Volume Contraction (whether 5-day average volume is below 20-day average volume).
How to interpret Score: The VCP Score multiplies the volatility compression and volume contraction ratios. A higher VCP Score means both volatility and volume have shrunk significantly near the highs, indicating a coiled spring setup ready to break out.

TradeKriya: Filter Interaction Guide

Our screener uses a strict disqualification model. If a stock triggers even one 🔴 Knife rule, it is classified as a Falling Knife. It must show 🟢 Safe across all active rules to be cleared.

200-Day SMA Filter

Checks the macro-level long-term trend direction. Is the stock trading above its 200-day simple average price?

🔴 Knife Price is below 200 SMA (long-term downtrend/bear market).
🟢 Safe Price is above 200 SMA (healthy structural uptrend).

Oversold RSI Filter

Measures short-term momentum speed and price exhaustion. Is daily 14-period RSI above the limit?

🔴 Knife RSI is below threshold (e.g. < 30). Stock is in severe panic capitulation.
🟢 Safe RSI is above threshold (e.g. ≥ 30). Panic has cooled or is stabilizing.

ADX Trend Strength

Measures trend strength (ADX) and direction (+DI vs -DI). Is there an active strong downtrend?

🔴 Knife ADX is > 25 and -DI > +DI. Bears are in strong momentum control.
🟢 Safe ADX is < 25 (ranging bottom) or +DI > -DI (bullish reversal).

Max Drawdown Filter

Measures peak-to-trough percentage drop from the 1-year (250-trading-day) high.

🔴 Knife Drawdown exceeds threshold (e.g., > 30%). Deep structural damage.
🟢 Safe Drawdown is within limits (e.g., ≤ 30%). Standard, healthy pullback.
