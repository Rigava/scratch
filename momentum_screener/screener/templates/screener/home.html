Falling Knife Setup (High Risk)

Stock falls vertically (Drawdown > 30%) below its 200-day moving average. Entering early leads to severe capital depletion.

Safe Momentum Setup (Low Risk)

Stock holds above its 200 SMA, consolidating with low drawdown levels (< 15%) and crosses oversold RSI zones. Perfect entry trigger.

Knife vs. Safe Setup

Never buy on the way down. Spot the shift.

A stock that is down 30% might look cheap, but buying without confirmation is a gamble. TradeKriya screens for peak-to-trough drawdowns and 200 SMA crossovers so you only trade when the trend shifts in your favor.
