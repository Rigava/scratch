TradeKriya Advisory Desk Guide

1. How to Create Trade Recommendations

Admins have two ways to generate institutional recommendations:

  • Autonomous AI Proposal (Gemini 3.8 Flash): Click "AI Proposal Generator", enter a stock symbol (e.g. TATASTEEL, RELIANCE), and choose a strategy. Gemini evaluates the price chart, fetches Google News RSS, drafts a thesis, renders an mplfinance chart with the TradeKriya badge, and puts the setup in "AI Drafts & Queue" for review.
  • Manual Trade Setup: Click "Manual Trade Setup", specify your exact entry range, targets, and stop-loss. An annotated chart will be auto-generated or you can attach your own screenshot.

2. Trade Lifecycle & Risk Management

  • Draft: Review pending. Visible only to research admins.
  • Active: Published live to subscribers.
  • Target 1 Hit: Partial profit booked. Admin trails stop loss to entry price (Risk-Free).
  • Closed in Profit: Target reached. Gains are recorded in the client-facing track record ledger.
  • Stop Loss Hit: Position closed at stop loss. Auditable record logged.

3. Dynamic Strategy Tuner

Admins can dynamically calibrate traditional moving averages (e.g., fine-tuning 50/200 SMA to 45/195 SMA for Indian equities) or RSI bands in the "Strategy Criteria Tuner" tab.
